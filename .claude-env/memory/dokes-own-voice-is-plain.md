His own messages, 2026-10-08:

    we have the path to QA and Prod . Ill wire up Dev now
    Also DNS fixed
    In Dev
    Puhsing to QA and Prod now

And Tim's, the same register:

    Also, I'm using a .env file for all my variables.  What is the requirement
    or method to get those injected?
    or are we 100% using the RisingWave secrets?
    I think the only thing I'm missing to push this branch is secrets.

**Why:** a drafted message that reads nothing like him does not get sent, or gets rewritten.
These are Teams messages between engineers who already share the context — the scaffolding I
add (headers, bold, tables, a framing sentence before the point) is weight for a reader who
does not need it, and it makes him sound like someone else.

**How to apply.** When drafting anything he will send to Tim, Idris or the team:

* One idea per line. Short lines. No headers, no bold, no tables.
* Lead with the fact. No "Two answers for you" preamble.
* Say the thing and stop. No closing offer unless there is a real one.
* Typos and missing capitals are his, not errors to correct into formality.

**Scope:** this is his outbound voice. It does not change how I answer *him* — that stays
decision-first per [[explain-simply-for-delegation]] — and it does not apply to PR bodies,
review comments or notes, which are read by people who were not in the conversation.

A long draft is usually a sign I have not decided what the point is. Cut it to the lines he
would actually type.
