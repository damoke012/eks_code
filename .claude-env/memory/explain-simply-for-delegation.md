Doke's job is to **assign work and brief people**, not to hold the detail. An answer he
cannot repeat to Tim, Idris or a manager in two minutes has failed, however correct it is.

**Why:** on 2026-08-26 he said *"we went from saying we need to containerize and then to we
don't need to containerize and right now I don't even know how to assign task to Idris."*
Every individual answer had been accurate. The volume and the reversals left him unable to
act — which is the only thing the answers were for.

**How to apply:**
* **Lead with the decision or thestatus, not the reasoning.** One sentence, then detail only
  if asked.
* **Say what changed and why in one line** when correcting an earlier position. No
  re-litigating. "That was wrong because X — here's what's true now."
* **Every status ends with who does what next.** A finding with no owner is not finished work.
* **Separate the ask from the discoveries.** "You asked for A. Doing A surfaced B, C, D."
  Otherwise scope creep reads as confusion.
* **Default to a table or a short list.** Prose paragraphs are for nuance he asked for.
* Reserve long explanations for when he says "explain in detail" — and even then, structure
  it so the first line of each section is the takeaway.
